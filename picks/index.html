Every clip reads the same script and plays at the same loudness (-16 LUFS), so you are only comparing the sound. Tap Pick on one voice and one music option, then Copy and send it back.

Recommendation: V1 (Microsoft Ava Multilingual) over M1 (Gymnopedie No 1) with light sound design. Ava speaks at a natural 177 words a minute with a warm, varied pitch, a transcription check caught every word, it is free with no key and takes seconds on the server. The current Chatterbox voice (V9) rushes at 234 words a minute, which is a big part of why it sounds made. If you want even more human, the next step is Google's Gemini voice (free key, 5 minutes to set up).
